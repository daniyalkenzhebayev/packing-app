Privacy Policy for Packlist

Last updated: 12.09.2026

Packlist ("the app") is a packing and inventory checklist application. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Email address — used to create and secure your account.
  • Trip, category, and item data — the packing lists you create.
  • Photos — images you optionally attach to items, stored to help you identify what to pack.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide the app's functionality: storing your packing lists, enabling you to log in securely, and allowing you to share trips with people you invite. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a third-party database and authentication provider. Access to your data is restricted using database-level security rules so that only you, and people you explicitly invite to a shared trip, can view or edit it.

Sharing Trips

If you invite another person to a shared trip, that person will be able to view and edit the trip's categories, items, and photos. You can remove a person's access at any time.

Data Deletion

You may delete your trips, categories, and items at any time within the app. To request full account deletion, contact us at [d.kenzhebaev2007@gmail.com].

Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase (database, authentication, file storage)
  • Vercel (web hosting)

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
